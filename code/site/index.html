A note for you

Dear Tanisha,

I made you something a little unusual. It isn't flowers and it isn't a card. It's a whole language, small enough to fit in your pocket, and it carries your initials.

I know code can look like a wall of strange symbols. TB is my attempt to make it feel more like writing a note: you say something, and the computer says something kind back. You can't break anything, and every mistake comes with a gentle hint.

There's no rush to learn any of it. Open it on a quiet evening, run the first program, and know that every line was written thinking of you.

With love, always.

What is TB?

TB is a small, gentle programming language. You type instructions in plain words like say and ask, and TB follows them, one line at a time.

It runs on your laptop as a single file, with nothing else to install. If you would rather not install anything yet, the playground runs TB right here in the browser.

When you're ready to learn, the guide starts from the very beginning.

A small taste of TB

let name = "Tanisha"
say("Hello, " + name + "!")

for word in ["kind", "brave", "funny"] {
  say(name + " is " + word + ".")
}

care(name)
